# E-road continuity audit

Every road, walked end to end.  A break is only a defect when the treaty says the road runs through and the data says it does not; sea links the AGR writes as `...` are real gaps.

- Roads audited: **228**
- Single connected path: **176** (77%)
- In more than one piece: **52**

| break | meaning | count |
|---|---|---|

| ferry | AGR sea link with a ferry across it | 3 |
| touching | under 50 m apart | 1 |
| defect | **AGR says road; data says gap - ours to fix** | 138 |
| unknown | could not be measured | 0 |
